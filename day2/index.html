\note:B4
Sing the next word on this pitch. Accepts C#5, Eb3 and Italian names such as SI4, sol3.
\note:G4,A4
One note per syllable. Extra notes become a melisma on the last syllable.
\time:4
Duration of the next word: 1 whole, 2 half, 4 quarter, 8, 16. Use 4. for dotted and 2+8 for tied notes, or 8,8 for one value per note. A sung word with no time reuses the last one.
\rest:4
Inserts a silence of that duration.
\tempo:120
Quarter notes per minute. Stays active until changed.
\lang:en
Switches language mid-text (it or en).
