How to play

The cylinder has six columns and three rings. There are 17 tiles: three in each colour, two in white, so one slot is always empty.

Goal: six columns of a single colour each.

  • Turn a ring: drag a tile left or right; the ring snaps every 60°. Keys: Q / W top, A / S middle, Z / X bottom.
  • Slide a tile: click (or drag up / down) a tile right above or below the gap. Keys: ↑ / ↓.
  • Look around: drag outside the cylinder (up / down tilts the view), use the mouse wheel, ⟲ ⟳ or ← / →.
  • Scramble (N) mixes the cylinder with 40 random moves, Undo (U) takes back a move, Rewind plays everything backwards to the solved state.
  • L switches between light and dark mode, M turns the sound effects on and off, B the music.
  • Hint (I) plays the next step of the computer's solution, Solve lets the computer solve the cylinder from where it is.

Every 60° step of a ring counts as one move, every slide counts as one move.
